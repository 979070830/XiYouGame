隱私政策

《悟空·十萬八千里》 · 更新日期:2026-07-31

一、我們收集的信息

本遊戲爲單機遊戲。你的遊戲進度(通關記錄、星星、法寶、簽到等)僅保存在你的設備本地(localStorage / 應用沙盒),不會上傳到我們的服務器。

二、我們不做什麼

· 不要求註冊賬號,不收集姓名、手機號、郵箱等個人身份信息。
· 不讀取你的通訊錄、相冊、位置等敏感權限。
· 遊戲核心玩法不依賴網絡,離線可玩。

三、第三方服務

iOS 版本中以下系統 / 第三方能力可能涉及數據處理:

· Apple App Store 內購:購買世界解鎖、星星禮包時,支付由 Apple 處理,我們僅接收"購買成功"的結果,不接觸你的支付信息。
· Game Center(可選):排行榜成績會提交至 Apple Game Center,受 Apple 隱私政策約束,可在系統設置中隨時退出登錄。
· AdMob 激勵廣告(可選):僅在你主動點擊"看廣告復活 / 翻倍 / 抽獎"時展示。廣告 SDK 可能依據 Apple《App 跟蹤透明度》徵得你的同意後收集設備標識符用於廣告歸因。
· 本地通知(可選):每日簽到提醒,僅在你的設備本地調度,不經過服務器。

四、數據刪除

刪除 App 即可清除全部本地遊戲數據。遊戲內不提供賬號體系,無服務器側數據需要刪除。

五、兒童隱私

本遊戲面向全年齡,不主動收集任何兒童個人信息。

六、政策變更

如接入新的第三方服務,我們會在本頁面更新清單並重新標註更新日期。

七、聯繫我們

對本政策有疑問,可通過 App Store 頁面提供的開發者聯繫方式與我們溝通。

本政策的中文版本與英文翻譯如有歧義,以中文版本爲準。

Privacy Policy

Wukong: 108,000 Li · Last updated: 2026-07-31

1. Information We Collect

This is an offline single-player game. Your progress (clears, stars, relics, check-ins) is stored locally on your device only (localStorage / app sandbox) and is never uploaded to any server of ours.

2. What We Don't Do

· No account registration; we never ask for your name, phone number, or email.
· No access to your contacts, photos, location, or other sensitive permissions.
· Core gameplay works fully offline.

3. Third-Party Services

The iOS build may involve the following system / third-party capabilities:

· Apple In-App Purchase: when you buy world unlocks or star packs, payment is processed entirely by Apple. We only receive a "purchase succeeded" result and never see your payment details.
· Game Center (optional): leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple Game Center, governed by Apple's privacy policy; you can sign out anytime in system Settings.
· AdMob Rewarded Ads (optional): shown only when you explicitly tap "watch ad to revive / double / draw". Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency, the ad SDK may collect device identifiers for ad attribution only with your consent.
· Local Notifications (optional): a daily check-in reminder, scheduled locally on your device with no server involved.

4. Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes all local game data. There is no account system and no server-side data to delete.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If new third-party services are integrated, we will update the list on this page and refresh the date above.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, reach us via the developer contact on our App Store page.
